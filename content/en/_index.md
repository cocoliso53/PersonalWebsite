# Cuau Cortés

I'm a developer and two-time founder based in Mexico City. I enjoy learning about prehispanic history and competing in track and field events.

## Startups founded


### Suarmi (RIP 2024)

Crytpo <> fiat ramp for the Mexican market connected directly into Mexican banking rails. Pretty innovative at the time since the only options were P2P or  credit/debit cards.
We made buying and selling crypto in Mexico fast and affordable. I built the entire tech stack using clojure, clojurescript, datomic, a bit of python and vanilla js.

### Resuave (exit 2021)

Sending money from the US/Europe to Mexico is solved, Wise, Remitly, etc.—the list goes on. Turns out sending money FROM Mexico to other countries was super expensive and slow. 
We founded Resuave in 2020 to tackle that problem. It was acquired a year after working on it. Here's where I first picked my clojure based stack.

## Current Projects

I've been leveragins LLMs to learn more about hardware and electronics, and since getting a 3D printer now I'm not limited to software when coming up with products or solutins. These are some of the things I'm working at the moment: 

### PlanPlanPlants

I like plants. My plants are alive and well but they are not blooming as I think they could be blooming. I want to track how healthy they are in order for them 
to grow beautiful. Currently working on v2 of this, v1 was completed and left a lot of learnings. 

### TZeroHero

Measuring performance is essential for progress, sprinting is no different. My main issue right now is that I run in a fairly busy track. There are plenty of solutions to measure 
speed in track and field events but they are either way too [expensive](https://freelap.com/athletics) or just not good enough for the conditions I train in.

I'm building an ESP32 mini + Raspberry pi system to measure my sprinting times. 

### TrackThrow

Pretty much the same issue I have for TZeroHero, since I'm also a thrower I need a device or something that can tell me how far I throw in each workout.
